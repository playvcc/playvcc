Valorant Console Circuit

Organized by SiN — Strength in Numbers

VCC is a competitive console Valorant ecosystem built for players, teams, creators, and organizations that want a structured path from open competition to elite championship play.

Players create profiles, captains build teams, rosters are controlled through invites, and teams earn Pro Points through weekly cups, majors, qualifiers, and championship events. VCC gives console players a real league hub with rankings, player history, team pages, free agent discovery, and scrim matchmaking.

How VCC Works

1. Players sign up with email, username, and password. Emails stay private.

2. Players complete profiles with Riot ID, role, platform, bio, and free agent status.

3. Captains create teams and invite players by username.

4. Players accept invites, rosters lock, teams compete, and rankings update.

Official Features

Leaderboard: teams, players, and free agents are separated into clean ranking pages.

One-team lock: players can only be on one active roster at a time.

Scrim queue: captains queue teams and the site auto-matches another team waiting in the same division.

Live backend: teams, rosters, invites, and scrims pull from Supabase.
