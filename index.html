About VCC

Official Hub

The Valorant Console Circuit is built to give console players a real competitive structure. Teams can create rosters, queue for scrims, compete in tournaments, submit results, and climb through the VCC ecosystem.

Preseason League

Premier-style weekly matches for seeding and ranking teams.

Pro Point Qualifiers

Teams earn points through placements and consistent results.

Group Stages

Group A/B/C/D pools with teams advancing into playoffs.

Match Rooms

Map bans, server bans, score submissions, and team chat.

Main Rules

Full Rules
  • Console only: players must compete on approved console platforms.
  • No cheating: XIM, macros, exploits, account sharing, and smurf abuse are prohibited.
  • Roster lock: teams must follow roster lock deadlines for events and playoffs.
  • Score reporting: both captains submit scores. Matching scores auto-approve. Mismatches become disputes.
  • Respect staff: admin decisions protect competitive integrity and league quality.
