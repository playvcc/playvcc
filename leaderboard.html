About VCC

Official Hub

The Valorant Console Circuit is built to give console players a real competitive structure. Teams can create rosters, queue for scrims, compete in tournaments, submit results, and climb through VCC.

Preseason League

Premier-style weekly matches for seeding and ranking teams.

Pro Point Qualifiers

Teams earn points through placements and consistent results.

Group Stages

Group A/B/C/D pools with teams advancing into playoffs.

Match Rooms

Map bans, server bans, score submissions, and team chat.

Main Rules

Full Rules
  • Console only: approved console platforms only.
  • No cheating: XIM, macros, exploits, account sharing, smurf abuse prohibited.
  • Roster lock: teams must follow event deadlines.
  • Score reporting: matching captain scores auto-approve; mismatches dispute.
