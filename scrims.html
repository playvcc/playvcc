VCC Competition Rules

Rules

Official VCC rules, roster policies, player conduct, match reporting, and competitive integrity.

Main Rules

Console OnlyPlayers must compete on approved console platforms.
No CheatingXIM, macros, exploits, account sharing, smurfing, and stat manipulation are prohibited.
Roster LockTeams must follow roster lock deadlines before playoffs or major events.
Score ReportingBoth captains submit scores. Matching scores auto-approve. Mismatches become disputes.
SportsmanshipToxicity, harassment, or hate speech may result in suspension or removal.
Competitive IntegrityAdmins may review screenshots, VODs, stats, rosters, and match evidence.

Tournament Structure

Preseason LeagueWeekly premier-style matches for seeding and rankings.
Group StageTeams are split into groups with round-robin play.
PlayoffsTop teams advance into single or double elimination brackets.
Pro PointsTeams earn points based on placements, wins, and event performance.
