VCC Official Rulebook

Select a division below to view the official rules and eligibility requirements.

VCC Elite Rules

Eligibility: VCC Elite is intended for high-level console Valorant competitors. Players must be Immortal rank or higher.

League Purpose

VCC Elite is the top competitive division in the Valorant Console Circuit. This division is designed for the strongest console teams competing for championships, Pro Points, major event qualification, and recognition as elite console competitors.

Player Requirements

  • Players must compete on console
  • Players must be Immortal or higher
  • Players must use legal peripherals only
  • Players must be registered on the official roster
  • Players may only play for one active team at a time

Team Requirements

  • 5 starting players required
  • Up to 2 substitute players allowed
  • Official team name and tag required
  • Team captain required
  • Roster must be approved by VCC staff

Competitive Format

  • Weekly Cups
  • Major Events
  • Championship Playoffs
  • BO3 regular matches
  • BO5 Grand Finals

Prohibited

  • Cheating
  • XIM / unauthorized adapters
  • Smurfing
  • Account sharing
  • Stream sniping
  • Fake score reporting
  • Toxic harassment

VCC Contenders Rules

Eligibility: VCC Contenders is intended for players who are Ascendant and under.

League Purpose

VCC Contenders is the official development division of the Valorant Console Circuit. This division is designed for rising players, newer teams, free agents, and organizations looking to compete and eventually qualify into VCC Elite.

Player Requirements

  • Console only
  • Ascendant rank or lower
  • One active team maximum
  • Must follow all VCC competitive rules
  • Must provide accurate account and roster information

Promotion System

  • Top performing teams may receive promotion opportunities
  • Staff may review eligibility and competitive placement
  • Teams dominating Contenders may be moved into Elite
  • Promotion may be based on Pro Points, playoffs, or staff review

Competitive Features

  • Open tournaments
  • Scrim queue support
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Team profiles
  • Free agent recruitment

Prohibited

  • Rank manipulation
  • Smurfing to stay in Contenders
  • Cheating or unauthorized adapters
  • Fake score reporting
  • Harassment or toxic behavior

WCC — Women's Console Circuit

Eligibility: WCC is intended for women competitors and follows protected-participation standards similar to Valorant Game Changers-style eligibility.

League Purpose

WCC exists to support and grow women's competitive console Valorant by creating a dedicated competitive environment, tournaments, showcases, and opportunities for women players and women-led teams.

Eligibility & Verification

  • Players must meet WCC eligibility requirements
  • WCC is limited to women competitors who fall under Valorant VCT/Game Changers-style participation terms
  • Staff may request verification if eligibility concerns arise
  • Fraudulent participation may result in removal or bans
  • All eligibility reviews are handled privately and respectfully

Competition Rules

  • Console only competition
  • 5 player starting roster
  • Up to 2 substitutes
  • BO3 regular matches
  • BO5 Finals
  • One active team maximum

Prohibited

  • Cheating
  • Harassment
  • Fake registration information
  • Competitive manipulation
  • Toxic or discriminatory behavior
  • Unauthorized adapters
