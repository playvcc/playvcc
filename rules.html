Official VCC Rulebook

Rules

Official Valorant Console Circuit rules for Elite, Contender, Academy, and Women’s Division play.

Universal VCC Rules

Platform Eligibility

  • VCC is console-first. Players must compete on approved console platforms.
  • XIM, Cronus, macros, recoil scripts, unauthorized adapters, and input spoofing are banned.
  • Players must use their own Riot account and may not account share.

Competitive Integrity

  • No cheating, exploiting, stream sniping, match fixing, stat padding, or intentional throwing.
  • Admins may request screenshots, VODs, scoreboard proof, or match room logs.
  • False evidence or lying in disputes can result in suspension or permanent ban.

Score Reporting & Disputes

  • Both captains must submit the final score after each match.
  • If both captains submit matching scores, the result auto-approves and updates the site.
  • If scores do not match, the match becomes disputed and is placed on hold.
  • Captains must provide screenshots or clips when requested by staff.
  • Repeated false disputes may result in suspension, point removal, or permanent ban.

Elite Division

Immortal+
  • Designed for Immortal, Radiant, and top-tier console competitors.
  • Players may be required to show rank proof before roster approval.
  • Roster lock applies before playoffs and major events.
  • Elite results carry the highest VCC pro-point value.
  • Stricter evidence review may be used for disputes and stat approval.

Contender Division

Open Division
  • Open to players below Elite requirements and teams still building experience.
  • Players may move into Elite if their rank or results qualify them.
  • Contender events award VCC points at a lower scale than Elite.
  • Smurfing to avoid Elite placement is prohibited.

Academy Division

Development
  • Built for newer teams, prospects, substitutes, and developing rosters.
  • Academy players may be called up to main rosters if roster rules allow.
  • Coaching, vod review, and player development are encouraged.
  • Academy teams must still follow all anti-cheat and sportsmanship rules.

Women’s Division

Women’s Circuit
  • Designed to support and grow women’s console Valorant competition.
  • Harassment, targeted toxicity, or identity-based abuse results in immediate staff review.
  • Roster eligibility may be reviewed by admins if needed to protect division integrity.
  • Women’s Division events may have separate standings, pro points, and finals.

Tournament Structure

Preseason League Mode

  • Weekly matches used for seeding, ranking, and division evaluation.
  • Teams build early records before official points-heavy events.
  • Admins may adjust seeding based on records, score differential, and activity.

Group Stage + Playoffs

  • Teams may be split into groups such as Group A, B, C, and D.
  • Top teams from each group advance into playoffs.
  • Tiebreakers may include match record, map differential, round differential, head-to-head, then admin ruling.

Conduct & Penalties

  • Minor violations may result in warning, match forfeit, or temporary suspension.
  • Major violations may result in season ban, team removal, point reset, or permanent ban.
  • Admins have final authority on disputes, eligibility, bracket issues, and emergency rulings.
  • VCC reserves the right to update rules to protect competitive integrity.
